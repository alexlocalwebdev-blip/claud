## Leg 1 — Luke Reynolds OVER receptions (VT vs Pitt)

**Player profile**
- 25 catches, 265 yards, 4 TD through 3 games (8.3 catches per game).
- 12 catches for 107 yards and a TD vs Maryland. Back-to-back 100-yard games, a first for a Virginia Tech tight end.
- Leads all Power Four tight ends in receptions, yards and touchdowns.
- QB Ethan Grunkemeyer is 79-of-101 (78.2%), 865 yards, 7 TD, 0 INT. Short, accurate distribution funnels to the tight end.

**Team matchup (Pitt defense)**
- Pitt's run defense is legit: held UCF to 20 rushing yards and 206 total yards, with 3 sacks and 8 TFL.
- Pitt's pass defense is the softer side: Miami (OH)'s McComb still threw for 240 on Pitt in a blowout, and Pitt was allowing 289 ypg before the UCF game.
- If Pitt bottles up Jeffrey Overton Jr. on the ground, Virginia Tech throws more, and Reynolds is the hot-read outlet against Pitt's pressure.
- WR Ayden Greene was evaluated for an injury at Maryland. Any limitation pushes more targets to Reynolds.

**Risk:** a lopsided game either way trims second-half volume. Pitt's pass rush forces quick throws, which actually favors tight-end catches.

## Leg 2 — James Peoples OVER rushing yards (PSU at Northwestern)

**Player profile**
- Career-high 67 yards on 14 carries vs Wisconsin; 59 yards vs Buffalo the week before. Trending up.
- He is the lead back right now: Quinton Martin Jr. (10 for 100 vs Buffalo) has missed two straight games with an undisclosed injury and no announced return date.
- Penn State's passing game is thin: WR1 Koby Howard (12 catches, 344 yards) is out for an "extended period," and Rocco Becht was 12-of-29 for 129 yards vs Wisconsin. Coach Matt Campbell has every reason to lean on the run.

**Team matchup (Northwestern defense)**
- Northwestern just allowed 271 rushing yards to Indiana (Lee Beebe 24 for 203).
- Penn State is a 7-point road favorite (total 48.5). A lead means a run-heavy fourth quarter.

**Risk:** Martin Jr. returns and splits carries; Becht (20 career rushing TDs) vultures goal-line work; the Carson Hansen / Cam Wallace rotation eats a few touches. Yardage still favors Peoples as long as Martin is out.

## Leg 3 — Aidan Chiles anytime TD (Northwestern vs PSU)

**Player profile**
- 4 rushing TDs in 3 games: a designed draw vs South Dakota State, two vs Colorado, one vs Indiana.
- Chip Kelly's offense uses Chiles on designed red-zone and short-yardage runs. That is the exact kind of role that produces touchdowns regardless of passing yardage.
- Passing: 273 / 241 / 293 yards, 73% completions, 0 INT. He is the whole offense.

**Team matchup (Penn State defense)**
- This is the tough leg. Penn State ranks 4th nationally in total defense (201 ypg) and 2nd against the pass (90 ypg).
- But Penn State just gave up 24 points to Wisconsin, and Northwestern's implied team total (about 20.75) means 2–3 scoring chances. Chiles is the most likely Wildcat to punch one in.
- New Ryan Field opener, Friday night, national TV: Northwestern will empty the playbook.

**Risk:** Penn State's front dominates and Northwestern kicks field goals. If the price is worse than +110, swap in the alternate below.
