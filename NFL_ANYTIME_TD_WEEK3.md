## Game-by-game board

### Ravens vs Cowboys (Rio de Janeiro), 4:25pm ET, CBS — BAL -3.5, total 52.5 (highest of the week)
- **Derrick Henry (-225).** The anchor of the slate. 40 carries, 212 yards, 4 TD. Dallas has allowed the most rushing yards in the league and is missing LB DeMarvion Overshown, CB Cobie Durant, and safeties Malik Hooker and P.J. Locke. Expensive, but the most likely scorer on the board.
- **CeeDee Lamb (about +110 to +130).** Dak's top red-zone target, 2 TDs last week. With a 52.5 total, Dallas will have to score to keep pace. Best value on the Cowboys side.
- **Mark Andrews (about +125).** 10 catches, 98 yards, no TD yet. Regression candidate in a high-total game.
- **Jake Ferguson (about +200).** Two red-zone TDs last week, but 2 catches for 6 yards the week before. TD-or-bust dart.
- **Zay Flowers is active** but reportedly limited to 20–30 snaps. Skip his TD prop.
- **Fade: Javonte Williams.** 12 carries for 30 yards last week; the Ravens allow 3.6 yards per carry.

### Raiders at Saints, 4:25pm ET, CBS
- **Ashton Jeanty (+104).** Leads the league in red-zone RB touches (9 red-zone touches, 4 catches inside the 20), but the Raiders' offensive line is not run-blocking. Only playable at plus money.
- **Brock Bowers** is questionable (knee). If he is active, he becomes a target hog, but confirm before betting.
- **Alvin Kamara** props moved sharply down this week (rushing line 32.5 to 27.5), which is a warning sign. Travis Etienne Jr. is the Saints' other back. Pass on the Saints backfield.

### Vikings at Buccaneers, 4:05pm ET, FOX — MIN -1.5, total 42.5 (already kicked off)
- **Bucky Irving (about +140).** Red-zone volume as a runner and receiver; Vikings allow about 100 rushing ypg.
- **Aaron Jones (about +125)** is the other model favorite. Low total, so keep it to one leg from this game.

### Cardinals at 49ers, 4:05pm ET, FOX (already kicked off)
- **Christian McCaffrey (-204 to -225).** Chalk.
- **Kaelon Black (+275).** Has more carries than McCaffrey (21 vs 20) but no TD yet. Value dart.
- **Trey McBride (+170).** Arizona's red-zone target.

### Rams at Broncos, 8:20pm ET, NBC — LAR -2.5, total 44.5
- **Kyren Williams (-105 to -113).** Goal-line back who scored in both games, but Blake Corum had the same 12 carries last week. Fine as a leg, slightly overpriced.
- **Davante Adams (+110).** Nacua doubtful (McVay: "more unlikely than likely"). Adams was the entire passing game last week. Best value on SNF.
- **Blake Corum (+270).** Split is real; live dart.
- **Broncos side: Jaylen Waddle (+200), Courtland Sutton (+260), Evan Engram (+370).** Engram is the value play; linebacker is the weakest part of the Rams' coverage. Avoid the Broncos backfield: RJ Harvey, J.K. Dobbins and Jonah Coleman are a three-way committee (Coleman scored last week on 10 carries).
- Broncos home primetime games are 9-of-11 to the under, so limit yourself to two legs from this game.

### Eagles at Bears, Monday 8:15pm ET, ESPN — PHI -4.5, total 41.5
- **Saquon Barkley (-110 to -133).** Cleared, full practice. Bears are 22nd against the run. The Eagles will run to protect a lead against a Keenum-led Bears offense.
- **Jalen Hurts (about +140 to +150).** Tush push inside the 5 is a standing threat; a 41.7% model number is fair value at +140.
- **Bears: Caleb Williams is OUT (hamstring), Tyson Bagent in concussion protocol, Case Keenum starts.** Chicago scored 3 points last week. If you need a Bears leg: D'Andre Swift or Kyle Monangai (Monangai out-gained Swift last week, 47 to 45). Dart: Colston Loveland at +400 or better.
- **Fade Bears receivers** with Keenum under center and a 41.5 total.
